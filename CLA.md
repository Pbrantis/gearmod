# GearMod Individual Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft — pending legal review.** This document is a proposal and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It is not in force until this notice is removed.

You keep the copyright in your contribution. This agreement is a license, not a transfer of ownership.

## 7. How to agree

The project uses an automated CLA check on pull requests. On your first pull request, post this comment:

> I have read the GearMod CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your agreement covers all your future contributions to GearMod. The record of your signature (GitHub username, date and pull request) is kept by the project.

## Notes for the maintainer (remove before adopting)

- **Why a CLA and not a DCO:** a DCO (`Signed-off-by`) only certifies the origin of the code. It does not give you the right to relicense contributions under commercial terms. Since GearMod intends to sell its own editions, a license-grant CLA like this one is needed.
- **Tooling:** [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant) (hosted) or the `contributor-assistant/github-action` GitHub Action can enforce the check on every pull request and store the signatures.
- **Legal review:** confirm with a lawyer the governing law and jurisdiction clause (not included yet), the moral-rights clause under Brazilian law (Lei 9.610/98), and whether a separate corporate CLA is needed for contributions made on behalf of companies.
